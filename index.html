Yuning Qiu

I am currently a Research Scientist at RIKEN iTHEMS and RIKEN AIP, supported by RIKEN Quantum. I received my Ph.D. from Guangdong University of Technology (GDUT) in 2021 and was an intern at RIKEN Brain Science Institute (BSI) in 2016. Before joining iTHEMS, I worked as a researcher at RIKEN AIP through the Special Postdoctoral Researchers (SPDR) Program, starting in 2023.
