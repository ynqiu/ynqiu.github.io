About

Research Scientist at RIKEN iTHEMS and RIKEN AIP, Japan.

My research sits at the intersection of tensor networks, structured learning, and optimization. I am interested in turning mathematical structure into efficient algorithms for modern data and computational problems.

For code and reproducible implementations, see selected projects including BalancedTNN, NTRC, STEPS, and MORTRD.
