## Research

I am a **Research Scientist at RIKEN iTHEMS and RIKEN AIP, Japan**. My research develops tensor methods and efficient learning systems for structured data. I am interested in **tensor networks, machine learning, large language models, and quantum machine learning**.

My work explores how mathematical structure can make high-dimensional data analysis and learning more efficient, robust, and interpretable.
